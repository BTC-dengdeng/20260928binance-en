<h2 style="font-size:27px;line-height:1.5">9. How to Make Your First Binance Spot Trade</h2>

<p>If your account already has USDT, you can enter the spot market and use USDT to buy BTC.</p>

<p>A common path is:</p>

<p><strong>Binance App → Trade → Spot → BTC/USDT → Buy</strong></p>

<p>New users commonly use two order types.</p>

<h3 style="font-size:23px;line-height:1.6">Market Order</h3>

<p>A market order is filled against the best available prices in the order book.</p>

<p>Advantages include:</p>

<ul>
<li>Simple operation;</li>
<li>Usually faster execution;</li>
<li>No need to set your own price.</li>
</ul>

<p>However, market orders can experience slippage, so the actual average fill price may differ from the latest price shown when you submit the order.</p>

<h3 style="font-size:23px;line-height:1.6">Limit Order</h3>

<p>A limit order lets you set the price at which you are willing to buy BTC.</p>

<p>For example, if BTC is trading at 80,000 USDT but you only want to buy near 78,000 USDT, you can place a limit order and wait for the market to reach that level.</p>

<p>Limit orders provide more control over your entry price, but <strong>the order may never fill if the market does not reach your selected price</strong>.</p>
